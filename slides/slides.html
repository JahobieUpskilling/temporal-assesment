Juniper Salon · Prototype review

Filling last-minute openings
without the group-text scramble

Today a cancellation means Lena or Carla scan the Google Sheet, text several people from the salon phone, and try to remember who answered. Chairs stay empty during busy stretches, two people say yes to the same slot, and late replies cause confusion.

What hurts

  • Losing track of who was contacted and who declined
  • Forgetting to text the next person when it's busy
  • Several people texted at once → more than one "yes"
  • Late replies after someone else already accepted

What success looks like

  • Openings move to the next eligible person on their own
  • Never two people claiming the same slot
  • At a glance: who has the offer, time left, who's next
  • Goal: at least half of last-minute cancellations refilled
1 · The problemTemporal prototype for Juniper Salon

How the prototype behaves

One opening → one offer at a time → one winner

1 · CancellationStaff enter the opening: stylist, time, length. Square isn't touched.
→
2 · MatchWaitlist filtered by service length, availability and requested stylist, in the order they joined.
→
3 · OfferFirst person is texted. They get 15 minutes (configurable) to reply YES or NO.
→
4 · OutcomeYES → filled, others told. NO / no reply / skip → next person. Nobody left → staff told it's unfilled.

Rules Lena set

  • Only the person currently holding the offer can accept; a late or duplicate YES is politely refused
  • Staff can skip the current person or cancel the opening at any time
  • Offering stops when it's too close to the appointment (default 30 min; cutoff still Lena's call)
  • Winner's original booking is moved by staff in Square, by hand

Why Temporal matters here

  • Each opening is a durable Workflow: the 15-minute timer, the queue position and every reply survive restarts and crashes
  • Replies are Temporal Updates, so "first YES wins" is enforced in one place, not in a spreadsheet
  • Texts are retried automatically if the SMS provider hiccups
  • The dashboard is just a view of the Workflow's state: reload, reopen, still correct

Next step

A two-week trial on real cancellations

What we'd do

  • Connect a real SMS number (Twilio or Square Messages) with an inbound webhook, so clients reply by text, not a button
  • Read the live waitlist sheet instead of the copy
  • Send the filled / unfilled alert to the salon phone
  • Run it alongside the current process for two weeks; staff keep the final say in Square

What we'd measure

  • Share of last-minute cancellations refilled (target: ≥ 50%)
  • Minutes from cancellation to a confirmed YES
  • Double-acceptances (should be zero)
  • How often staff skip or cancel, to tune the reply window and the "too close" cutoff

Decisions needed from Lena: the "too close to the appointment" cutoff, and whether an accepted client stays on the waitlist.
